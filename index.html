Page is fixed at 16 steps — that's the one thing that never changes. Bars per Page is how many bars fit in those 16 steps at your current zoom level (4 bars/page if you're at quarter-note resolution in 4/4, 1 bar/page at the default 16th-note zoom). Set it to match how you actually work.
Section length is entirely up to you — there's no fixed size. Pages per Section just says how many pages long you want that chunk of the arrangement to be.
Bars Needed works backward from a target length using the same Bars per Page setting, so a drone's length shows up in pages too, not just bars.
Beats per bar scales the time-signature numerator against a quarter-note denominator (e.g. 6/8 = 3 quarter-note beats per bar), so duration stays accurate for compound meters.
Works offline once loaded — bookmark it on your phone.

Anatomy of the Deluge

Page is real Deluge terminology — it's the 16 steps of the pad grid visible on screen at once (default zoom). Scroll left/right and you move a full page at a time. It also shows up loosely for the OLED's parameter screens (filter page, envelope page), but on the pad grid it means those 16 visible steps specifically.
