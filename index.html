BPM and Zoom are the only two things that actually change on the hardware — there's no time-signature setting on the Deluge. BPM is the tempo dial; Zoom is what the ◄► knob controls, deciding how much musical time each of the 16 pad columns represents.
Step is the real unit of granularity — a clip's length can be set to any whole number of steps at your current zoom, down to a single step. That's why "Steps Needed" (not bars) is the number that matches what you can actually dial in.
Page is fixed at 16 steps — that never changes, regardless of zoom.
Bar, in the numbers below, is just the Deluge's own display convention: it always counts every 16 sixteenth-notes as one bar, for the numeric readout, regardless of your zoom or the meter you're actually playing in. It's shown here for reference, not because it's something you set.
